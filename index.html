THEMES

Periodic themes every 14 days.

Each PAXEL cycle opens around a new theme — from sweeping landscapes like Utopia to intimate human moments like Happiness and stark contrasts like Darkness. Every theme shapes the palette, the mood and the kind of scene that slowly emerges from the noise. No two themes repeat, and no two canvases within a theme ever settle the same way.

OWNERSHIP

The wait is the proof.

1

You start the canvas

A blank 14-day canvas is assigned to you, ready to begin filling in pixel by pixel.

2

It grows on its own clock

Pixels settle in over real days. You can check in, but you cannot speed it up.

3

You receive the certificate

On day 14 the canvas locks for good. A unique certificate of authenticity is issued, validating your patience and permanently tying the piece to you.
